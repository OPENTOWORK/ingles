3. Promotion rule for future feedback

• One isolated teacher preference → record first; do not automatically alter architecture.

• Repeated issue across exercises or a clear exam-mechanic violation → propose promotion to system rule.

• Human approval is required before changing a structural source of truth.

• Once promoted, record the destination layer and version in this register.

• Do not rely on Cursor conversation memory as persistence.
